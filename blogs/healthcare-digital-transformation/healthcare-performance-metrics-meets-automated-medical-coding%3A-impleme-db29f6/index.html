The intersection of Automated Medical Coding and Healthcare Performance Metrics represents one of the most promising frontiers in modern medicine. Automated Medical Coding has emerged as a transformative technology in healthcare. By analyzing vast amounts of medical data, these systems can identify patterns and provide insights that would be impossible for humans to detect manually.
